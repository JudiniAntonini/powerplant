Enter **0** for a category with no downtime; a blank cell means unknown, so any metric needing it shows N/A.\ With several units, hours are summed as unit-hours and capacity factor uses Σ(unit capacity × hours). Average capacity factor and availability are the mean of the yearly values that could be calculated.
