Notes and Calculation Methodology

Calculation Method

Operating Hours = Total Available Hours − Downtime Hours (used only when not supplied directly)
Availability Factor = Available Hours / Total Hours × 100
Capacity Factor = Actual Energy / (Installed Capacity × Total Hours) × 100
Average Annual Generation = 12-Year Generation / 12 (only when all 12 years carry generation)
Downtime = planned + maintenance + unplanned + other outage hours. Enter 0 for a category with no downtime; a blank cell means unknown, so any metric needing it shows N/A.
With several units, hours are summed as unit-hours and capacity factor uses Σ(unit capacity × hours). Average capacity factor and availability are the mean of the yearly values that could be calculated.

Data format

Edit plantData in the Data section of the script, or use Load data (JSON or CSV). Schedule items use startYear, startMonth, endYear, endMonth (months default to 1 and 12; year is shorthand for a single year). Performance rows use year, unit, totalHours, operatingHours, plannedOutageHours, maintenanceHours, unplannedOutageHours, otherDowntimeHours, availableHours, expectedGenerationMWh, actualGenerationMWh, status, activities, notes.
